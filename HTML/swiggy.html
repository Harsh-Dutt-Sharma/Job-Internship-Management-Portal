Product Designer

Core Responsibilites

  • End-to-End Product Design:Own the entire lifecycle of specific product features and charters from concept to launch. This includes establishing information architecture, building user flows, wireframing, interactive prototyping, and designing high-fidelity interfaces.
  • Problem Structuring & Systems Thinking:Deconstruct ambiguous, complex operational or logistical challenges into clear, system-level design flows rather than just visual screens. This means designing for edge cases, error states, and high-frequency user interactions
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Product Managers, Engineering teams, Operations, Data Science, and UX Research to align design frameworks with engineering constraints, business metrics, and project timelines.
  • Data-Informed User Advocacy:Perform "user immersions" and review data-driven feedback or metrics alongside the data team to understand the core customer journey and pain points, replacing assumptions with validated research insights.

Skills Preferred

  • User Research & Insights: Conducting user interviews, field immersions, and usability testing to uncover friction points in the hyper-local delivery journey.
  • Interaction & Interface Design: Creating intuitive information architecture, task flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity screen designs for complex mobile apps.
  • Systems & Analytical Thinking: Deconstructing intricate logistics, multi-party workflows (consumer, delivery partner, vendor), and handling diverse edge cases.
  • Design System Proficiency: Working with and scaling comprehensive Design Language Systems (DLS) to maintain visual consistency and speed up development.
  • Prototyping & Motion Design: Building interactive, low- to high-fidelity prototypes using tools like Figma, ProtoPie, or Framer to simulate real-world app micro-interactions.
  • Data Literacy: Analyzing product metrics, A/B testing results, and user drop-off data to make informed, objective design iterations.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partnering effectively with Product Managers, Data Scientists, and Engineers to balance business goals with technical constraints.

Stipend:120000Rs+HRA+DA
